# Observability and Incident Response Skill

## Instrument
- Structured logs with timestamp, severity, service, environment, tenant-safe reference, and correlation ID.
- Metrics for request rate, error rate, latency, queue depth, database pool, job failures, and provider delivery.
- Distributed traces across API, database, and background jobs where supported.

## Privacy
Do not place student names, phone numbers, addresses, credentials, or sensitive payloads in telemetry.

## Operations
Define service-level indicators, alert thresholds, on-call ownership, severity levels, incident timeline, customer communication, and post-incident review.
