# Academic Management Skill

## Model carefully
Academic year/session, term, grade/class, section, subject, subject assignment, class teacher, enrollment, promotion, and school calendar.

## Rules
- Distinguish a class/grade from a section and from a student's enrollment in an academic session.
- Preserve historical enrollment and class assignments; do not overwrite last year's data.
- Define promotion, retention, transfer, withdrawal, and re-admission workflows.
- Prevent duplicate active enrollment for the same student/session unless explicitly supported.
- Support school-specific naming and configurable academic calendars.
- Validate effective dates and prevent conflicting assignments.

## Deliverables
Domain glossary, ERD, lifecycle rules, endpoint contracts, and scenario tests.
