# Examinations and Results Skill

## Requirements
- Separate exam definition, subject assessment, marks entry, grading scheme, result calculation, and publication.
- Support configurable maximum marks, pass criteria, grade bands, absent/exempt states, and rounding.
- Version grading schemes or snapshot applied rules so historical results remain reproducible.
- Restrict marks entry and verification by assigned class/subject and workflow state.
- Maintain change history for marks after submission.
- Generate report cards from a consistent result snapshot.
- Prevent publication before required verification and approval.

## Tests
Boundary marks, missing subjects, absent students, rounding, re-evaluation, locking, and historical recalculation.
