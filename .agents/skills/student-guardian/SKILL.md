# Student and Guardian Management Skill

## Requirements
- Separate person identity, student profile, guardian/contact, and academic enrollment.
- Support multiple guardians and explicit relationship/contact permissions.
- Define required versus optional admission fields per school configuration.
- Track admission number uniqueness within the correct tenant/scope.
- Preserve historical records and use archival/withdrawal states rather than destructive deletion where appropriate.
- Restrict access to sensitive student data by role and purpose.
- Validate document uploads and restrict access to private files.

## Workflows
Admission, document verification, enrollment, guardian linking, transfer, withdrawal, re-admission, and profile correction.
